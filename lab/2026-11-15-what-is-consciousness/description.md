What is consciousness?

A 3-minute journey through abstract point-cloud worlds into the oldest open question in science: why does
the universe feel like something from the inside? From 86 billion neurons and their electrical storm, through
perception, memory and the hard problem of consciousness, to the question no one has answered yet.
