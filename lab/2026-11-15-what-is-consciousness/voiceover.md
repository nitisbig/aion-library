# DARK

Why does the universe feel like something... from the inside?

For billions of years, there was only matter, moving in the dark. Dust, gathering into stars. Nothing, as far as we know, was felt.

And then, somewhere, something began to notice.

# MATTER

Look closely at a brain, and you will find no feelings. Only cells.

Eighty-six billion neurons, each one reaching out to thousands of others. Tiny pulses of electricity race along their branches. Chemicals drift across the gaps between them.

There is nothing here but physics. Atoms, many of them forged in stars, obeying the same laws as everything else.

# BRAIN

And yet, from this electrical storm, something extraordinary appears. Three pounds of matter that sees the color of the sky, that tastes the sea, that remembers a voice it loved.

# JOURNEY

Inside, there is a world.

What you see is not the world itself, but a model, built in darkness, inside the skull. Light becomes signals. Signals become color, and depth, and a face.

Memories are not stored like photographs. Each time you remember, you rebuild the moment, a little differently.

Every thought you have ever had was a pattern, rising, and fading away.

# GAP

But here is the mystery.

We can trace every signal, map every pathway, measure every spike. And yet, nowhere in that map do we find the blue of the sky, or the feeling of being you.

Philosophers call this the hard problem of consciousness. How does electrical activity become experience? Why doesn't all of this simply happen in the dark?

# FLUX

Some think awareness emerges when information is woven together in just the right way. Others suspect it is something deeper, a property of the universe itself.

No one knows.

What we do know is that awareness never stands still. It forms, dissolves, and forms again. Much of the matter in you is replaced, and yet, somehow, you remain.

# END

Perhaps you are not a thing at all, but a process. A pattern the universe is running.

So ask yourself, one more time.

Is consciousness something the universe created? Or is it a way for the universe to experience itself?
