What is time?

A 2.5-minute journey through abstract point-cloud worlds, from the time we feel every day to the time physics describes. Einstein showed that time is woven together with space, and that it slows down with speed and gravity. GPS satellites have to correct for this every day. So does time really flow, or is the flow something the mind brings to it?
