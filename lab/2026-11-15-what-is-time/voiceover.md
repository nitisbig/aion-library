# DARK

What is time?

We feel it everywhere. In the beat of a heart. In the light that fades at evening. In the quiet space between one thought and the next.

And yet, the moment we try to hold it, it slips away.

# ROAD

We seem to travel along a single road. Behind us lies the past: fixed, remembered, out of reach. Ahead lies the future: open, unformed, not yet written.

And between them, a thin bright edge we call now.

# FABRIC

But physics tells a stranger story.

In Einstein's relativity, time is woven together with space into a single fabric, called spacetime. A moment is not only a when. It is a place, in four dimensions.

# BEND

And this fabric bends.

Move fast enough, and your clock ticks slower than the clocks you left behind. Sink deeper into gravity, and time itself runs slow.

This is not an illusion. Satellites above us must correct their clocks for it, every single day.

So there is no single now, shared by the whole universe. Each of us carries our own time.

# BLOCK

Some physicists go further. If past, present and future are all part of one four-dimensional block, then perhaps nothing truly passes.

Every moment simply is, laid out like the frames of a film.

# ARROW

And yet, we remember the past, never the future. Order gives way to disorder. Coffee cools. Stars burn out. And the arrow points only one way.

# END

So does time truly flow? Or is the flow something we bring to it: a story the mind tells, one moment at a time?

Perhaps the deepest question is not what time is.

But why it feels like anything at all.
