## Description

How do you find the best answer when you're held to a limit? Queen Dido had one ox hide to claim as much land as she could, and the idea that solves her problem drives physics, economics and machine learning. In this visual explainer we build Lagrange multipliers from scratch: contour maps, gradients and the tangency condition ∇f = λ∇g. Then we go further than most courses: what λ actually means (a shadow price, a force, even temperature), where the method breaks, and how it extends to inequality constraints with the KKT conditions.
