What the Matrix Exponential Actually Is

You know e^x. But what could e^A possibly mean when A is a matrix? Start with Bernoulli's compound interest, read a matrix as a velocity, and see e^{tA} as the trip that velocity takes you on. From there the series, eigenvectors, the trace, and a rule that breaks all fall out of the picture, and the same idea turns out to describe the medicine in your blood, a spinning planet and a quantum computer.
