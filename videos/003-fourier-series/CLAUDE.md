## The idea and its spine

- **One line:** a circle seen from the side is a wave, and enough circles stacked tip to tail can draw anything.
- **The question that runs the whole video:** *how can circles draw a corner?* The **square** carries it through every chapter (drawn square → square wave → hot/cold ring → rounded square → decoded → 1/n² vs 1/n).
- **The spectacle:** the **treble clef** (Noto Music, SIL OFL, U+1D11E, via `aion.drawings.treble_clef()`). The first thing on screen ("What you're looking at…"), rebuilt in Ch6, replayed in the finale.
- **The analogy:** a **piano chord** (one sound, several notes) is planted in the intro and paid off in Ch6 (singer vs violin).
- **The core (Ch5 ★):** to find a circle, turn the drawing against it and average. `c_n = ∫₀¹ f(t) e^{−2πint} dt`. This formula is ghosted in the intro at a fixed screen position and decoded in Ch5 **at that same position**.
- The promise tracker in `scene-plan.md` lists every open loop. Don't cut a payoff without cutting its setup.

## Decisions (keep unless the user changes them)

- **Look:** same as 002. Pure black stage, light only from thin geometry, two-level bloom, vignette 0.5, grain 0.04, Space Grotesk / Inter Light / Libertinus. Minimal text on screen.
- **Colors** (add to `aion/style.py` under `# Fourier series (video 003)`, aliasing 002 constants where the meaning matches): `INK` gold = pen + every wave/drawing · `FWD` blue = positive (counterclockwise) speed · `BWD` orange = negative speed · `STILL` green = speed zero, balance point, frozen circle, `c_n` · `PHASE` violet = starting angle · `ARM` silver = rims/arms · `SPIKE` red = Gibbs only · `HEAT_RAMP` for Ch3 only. Never use ad-hoc colors in scenes.
- **Engines:** Manim for ≤ 30 circles and all equations · moderngl `EpicyclePlate` for 50–500 circles, zoom, and the 2.5D finale · moderngl `HeatRingPlate` for Ch3 · Taichi `InkParticles` for pen sparks and flame/frost · pyvista for the Ch1 helix spring (fallback: Manim 3D) · skia for title, cards, starfield, image tiles · scipy DCT for the Ch6 compression demo.
- **Math is computed, never drawn:** all coefficients come from `aion/fourier.py` (FFT), which is unit-tested against the closed forms in `scene-plan.md` (square wave 4/(πn), square loop c₁/n² for n ≡ 1 mod 4, Gibbs 8.95 %, heat e^{−n²κt}). The Ch5 "bean" is built from 5 known circles so every on-screen answer is exact.
- **Determinism:** seed every RNG (starfield, particles, synthetic sounds, the noise in the teaser wave).

## Phase 4 gotchas (learned building the intro)

- **Every updater that must run during `wait()` needs a `dt` parameter** (`lambda m, dt: …`). Manim treats `lambda m:` updaters as static and freezes `wait()` into one repeated frame: S01's clef stopped drawing for its last 3 s until this was fixed. `V.VideoScene.drive()` and `EpicyclePlate` already do it.
- **Drive clocks statelessly:** `self.drive(tracker, fn(now_abs))`. Any frame renders alone, and cuts match because S(N+1) recomputes the same value at its first frame.
- **Don't pass `rate_func=` to `play()`/`beat()` when animations carry their own `V.window(...)` rate funcs**: the play-level one overrides them. Use `play_at()` without `rate_func`.
- `EpicyclePlate` is additive light on black: Manim mobjects under it stay visible. Its drawing coordinates are Manim coordinates, and `plate.project(z)` pins labels.
- The S02 heat-ring teaser is a Manim stand-in (`V.heat_ring`). Swap it for a still from `HeatRingPlate` once S11 exists.
- Build order (user decision): the whole intro first, then chapter by chapter.
- **Use `V.redraw(fn)`, never `always_redraw` or `become()` in a dt updater.** `become()` pads mismatched shapes (e.g. an empty `VMobject` ↔ a `DashedLine`) and the nesting grows every frame until a RecursionError. `redraw` swaps the holder's single child instead. That only works because `V.VideoScene.get_moving_mobjects` returns top-level mobjects: Manim caches the moving list for a whole `play()`/`wait()`, and the default flattened list kept drawing swapped-out children (a frozen doubled arm and wave).
- **Ch1 machine (`scenes/ch1.py`):** `OneCircle` draws circle/arm/track/wave from absolute time and a few trackers (`r, n, phi, tilt, grow, *_op, master`). The arm rests pointing right, eases to one turn per 4 s on "turning steadily", and is phased so the tip tops out on "up". Speed changes are anchored at `Tn` (the "its speed" beat) so the arm never jumps. States `S04_END`, `S05_END`, `FLAT` are the cut hand-offs; S07 starts from `FLAT`.
- **S06 pyvista (`scenes/spring_pv.py`):** frames cached in `media/cache/s06_<w>x<h>_<fps>/<hash>/` with `labels.json` (label anchors projected by VTK). Call `pl.render()` before `screenshot()`, or every frame comes back identical. About 3 fr/s at 480p, so a 1080p60 final takes roughly 10 min for the frames alone. S06 opens and closes with a 0.45–0.6 s dissolve between the flat picture and the 3D view (the two aren't one consistent projection).
- **Ch2 machine (`scenes/ch2.py`):** circle sizes, arrivals and the circle count are pure functions of time (`amps(t)`, `n_circles(t)`), and so is the chapter clock `clock(t)`: it equals real time until the guess, slows to 15 % on "take a moment to guess", recovers as S09 starts, and eases to a full stop as S10 starts. Every Ch2 cut matches by construction. The wave is always drawn from the current circles.
- S09 hands the chain over from Manim (≤ 4 circles) to `EpicyclePlate` (rims + arms only; Manim keeps pen, wave, counter, strip). With 200 circles the 2D chain whips far left at each jump (the conjugate series grows like log N). That's real math, so it's left in.
- S10 differs from the storyboard in two ways. N drops 200 → 10 during the zoom, so "more circles" can show 10 → 50 → 200. The zoom tracks the jump's x live while the clock stops, so the view never drifts. The red corner marker (`ch2.corner_marker()`) is at (6.3, 3.35) and must reappear there until S26.
- S07: the storyboard's "camera eases in slightly" on "two motions" is a rim brighten instead (Cairo has no camera move in a plain Scene).

- **Ch3 (`scenes/ch3.py`, `aion/components/mesh_gl.py`):** the "HeatRingPlate" is `MeshPlate` (moderngl: lit opaque tubes + additive glass ribbons + point sprites, stateless per frame). One tube is both ring and bar (`centerline(psi, v)`, arc length preserved), so temperature stays on the metal while it bends or unrolls. Temperature is exact (`modes()`: mode n decays as e^{−n²τ}); the drawn square start uses Lanczos σ-factors so the crown shows no Gibbs ripple (that belongs to Ch2). Flame/frost particles are hash-based functions of time (no Taichi needed). Python mesh building at 1080p will be slow (~1–5 fr/s at 480p now).
- S12 has two heat clocks (`tau_ramp`): the three mode rings start on "keeps its shape", the main ring on "sharp edges melt". The mode rings share one crown gain (`SMALL_GAIN`) so their 1 : 1/3 : 1/5 heights stay true. `scene_12_modes_fade.end_state(now)` rebuilds S12's last picture for S13.
- S13's jagged profile is a step function with exact closed-form coefficients; the sine column shows its true n = 1, 2, 3 terms at the same scale. It ends on `ch3.drawing_seed()` (one circle, arm pointing up, at (−4, 0.6)), which S14 starts from.
- The S02 heat-ring teaser is still the Manim stand-in; swapping it for a MeshPlate still is optional polish.
- **Ch4 (`scenes/ch4.py`):** chains are drawn in Manim (≤ 2 circles), rims colored by speed sign. Every motion runs on a `ch4.Clock` (an integrated, eased rate curve built from cue times), so all cuts match by construction. Clocks: `clock14` (S14–S15 chain), `years` (S15 sky), `S16.CLOCKS`, `big_clock` (the big circle's one solo turn, ending on "one ninth") and `friend_clock` (both circles from "backward").
- S14's chain is speeds 1 and 3 (sizes 1.2 and 0.3). S15's sky is real: Mars 1.524 AU / 1.881 yr on Earth's 1 AU / 1 yr, phased so an opposition (a retrograde loop) falls during the circles beat. The aside re-centers on the Sun (`sky_point(y, s)`). Stars are seeded Manim dots, not skia.
- S17 uses the intro square's own c₁ = 2.522 and c₋₃ = −c₁/9, so the dashed intro square (half side 2.2) fits around the rounded square exactly. S18 ends on `scene_18_rack.end_picture()` (the empty rack, speeds −5…5, plus the clef ghost); **S19 must start from that**.
- **Ch5 (`scenes/ch5.py`):** the spin-against view is a rotating frame (`ch5.Frame`): arms turn at n − k, and the ink is the pen's trace in that frame. **Φ must be ≡ 2πkL (mod 2π) whenever k is steady**, or the frozen arm sits at the wrong angle and the dots' mean misses its tip. A plain 2π∫k dL got this wrong; `Frame` eases each k change in with Λᵢ = round(L) so it stays exact.
- `beats.BEAN` was re-phased (cₙ·e^{0.6in}: the same curve, the loop started later) so c₁ has a real starting angle (0.6 rad) for S21's violet arc. S02's gallery uses the same curve, so it needs no re-render.
- In S21 the bean's chain is ordered by size (speed 1 first, from O), so the spin-average lands on the frozen arm's tip. S19 orders it speed 0 first (the still arm from O).
- The rule card (`ch5.card*`) is docked top-left (the storyboard said bottom-left; the rack sits at the bottom). The rack is S18's, scaled 0.65 along the bottom (`ch5.rack_items`).
- S23 ends on `scene_23_recipe.end_formula()` (the colored recipe and its two braces at center), the dimmed rack, the docked card, the eyebrow and the marker; **S24 must start from that**.
- **Ch6 (`scenes/ch6.py`):** the clef chain is `shared.clef_terms()` minus c₀ (exactly 0), so it is exactly 200 circles, at `CLEF_AT` × `CLEF_K`. S24's strip is the real 201 coefficients; the 1,000 dots are the recipe's own samples, wound by n. S25's counter (`n_circles`), loop clock (`loop_clock`), zoom (`zoom_amount`) and camera are pure functions of time, and `s25_state(now)` serves S24's end, S25 and S26's start, so both cuts match by construction. The camera keeps the 200-circle pen's screen offset shrinking as (1 − u), so the pen never leaves the frame mid-dive; during the 150-circle toggle the pen visibly drifts off it.
- A VGroup whose updater must run has to be in the scene itself: `LaggedStart(FadeIn(d) for d in dots)` adds only the children, so S24 re-adds the group after the fade.
- S26 compares 20 circles (not the storyboard's 10) with 200 at the same t: within 0.12 units (~60 % of the clef) lights gold, the rest flashes red; 10 circles matched only ~10 %. Panels: square wave bars = `square_wave_radii` (1/n), square loop bars = the intro square's own |cₙ/c₁| (1/n² exactly), oval = 3 circles. `scene_26_smooth_sharp.end_picture()` is S27's start.
- S27's photo is the clef rasterized at 128 px; 8×8 block DCT (`scipy.fft.dctn`, ortho), keep the biggest 10 % globally, rebuild with `idctn` (RMS error ≈ 0.003). The wipe is a per-frame numpy composite. The block's bars use a sqrt scale for legibility.
- **Killing renders:** never `pkill -f` a pattern that also appears in the same shell command line (it kills the calling shell, exit 144). Use `[x]`-style patterns and keep the relaunch in a separate call.
- **Outro (`scenes/outro.py`):** S28's four glass cards (`aion.components.cards.card_image`) hold live miniatures whose clocks start on their cue words (`outro.T()`), so S29 rebuilds S28's last frame with `S28.picture(now, END_VALS)`. Card 2 is the real square wave (4/(πn), 2 → 12 circles between "right sizes" and "sharp corners"), card 3 the intro square's c₁ and c₋₃, card 4 the bean with the whole world turned by −2πL, so its speed-one arm stands still at arg c₁. S28 and S29 render at ~1.5 s/frame at 480p (heavy Cairo), so plan finals in the background.
- S29's never-repeating wave is a sum of incommensurate sines (1, √2, π/2, …) under a slow envelope; its band peaks at those same frequencies, mirrored for negative speeds (a real signal), smoothed for display. S30 replays S01's `clef_state` with tilt 25° and a 7°/s yaw; `loop_t` closes the loop exactly at the end of "round" (beats.FINALE_CLOSE).
