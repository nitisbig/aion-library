Divergence and curl, explained visually. Divergence asks "is stuff leaving?" and curl asks "is stuff turning?" We see both with a drop of dye and a tiny paddle wheel, derive ∇·F and ∇×F from a tiny square, build up the divergence theorem and Stokes' theorem, and finish by reading Maxwell's equations. A vector calculus intuition guide for students.
