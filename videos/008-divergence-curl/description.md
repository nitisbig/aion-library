What do divergence and curl actually measure? Drop dust into a moving stream and watch two things happen: in some places it spreads apart, in others it starts to turn. Those are the two questions you can ask any point in a flow. Is stuff leaving? Is stuff turning? We measure each one with a drop of dye and a tiny paddle wheel, derive ∇·F and ∇×F from a tiny square, then add the squares up to get the divergence theorem and Stokes' theorem. Finally, we read Maxwell's equations as plain sentences.
